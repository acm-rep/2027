<p>Reproducibility is foundational to science and engineering: the ability to
    apply  experimental methods that produce consistent results enables us to
    understand our own work, build on each other's work, and have high
    confidence in the body  of scientific literature.
    The study of reproducibility is a discipline in its own right, and ACM REP
    is a venue for work that contributes to our understanding and  practices of
    reproducibility and replicability in computer science, as well as    other
    fields that depend on computing.                                            
</p>
