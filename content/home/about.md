Reproducibility is foundational to science and engineering: the ability to
apply  experimental methods that produce consistent results enables us to
understand our own work, build on each other's work, and have high
confidence in the body  of scientific literature.
The study of reproducibility is a discipline in its own right, and ACM REP
is a venue for work that contributes to our understanding and  practices of
reproducibility and replicability in computer science, as well as    other
fields that depend on computing.                                            

REP 2027 will be held July 12-15 in the brand-new [Price Computing and
Engineering Building](venue/) on the campus of the University of Utah in [Salt Lake
City](housing/).
We strongly encourage in-person participation to get the full
value of the conference, but will also offer remote options for those unable
to travel to the venue.

This year's event will focus on growing the REP community, [inviting a range
of work on the science and practice of reproducibility](cfp/), as well as
providing a [welcoming environment](conduct/) for [all](diversity).
It will feature a technical program, keynote speakers, a day of hands-on
tutorials, and events designed to form community ties that endure beyond the
conference.
