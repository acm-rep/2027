### Evaluation Steps

The process begins with artifact submission, followed by reviewer bidding and assignments, with expertise and conflicts of interest taken into consideration. This is followed by the kick-the-tires phase.

During the **kick-the-tires period**, reviewers check that they can access the artifact, set up its environment, and complete the initial check. Authors should respond to setup questions and help resolve problems that prevent evaluation during this phase.

During the **main evaluation period**, reviewers then assess the artifact against the required badges criteria. Here, authors and reviewers can communicate through the submission system and fix any problem that arise. Authors should document any fixes and identify updated versions there so reviewers can track what has changed. Reviewers should record the artifact version, steps performed, results obtained, and any claims that could not be evaluated.

After this, authors will receive the evaluation outcome and feedback. Unless an exception has been approved by the PC chairs, authors must archive the final artifacts and submit the persistent identifier before the end of the main evaluation period. Authors should also update the artifact appendix to reflect the evaluated version and include the archive link in the camera-ready paper.


### Review and Anonymity

The artifact evaluation is single-blind. Reviewers can see the authors' names and affiliations, while reviewer identities remain anonymous to authors. Reviewers must declare conflicts of interest before assignment.

All information shared during evaluation must be treated as confidential. Reviewers must not share artifact code, data, access credentials, evaluation outputs, or author discussions with AI tools or third-party services that do not guarantee confidentiality.

AI tools must not substitute for independent artifact evaluation or judgment. Reviewers must examine the artifact, carry out the relevant evaluation steps, and base their findings and badge recommendations on evidence they have checked themselves. 

The use of AI tools to improve the grammar, clarity, and presentation of reviewer-written text is permitted, provided confidentiality is preserved. Reviewers remain fully responsible for the accuracy and content of their reviews and badge recommendations.

We encourage all reviewers to read [this article](https://sysartifacts.github.io/evaluator-guide.html) for best practices and guidance on reviews.
