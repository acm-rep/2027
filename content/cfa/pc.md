### Nominations for AEC

We invite nominations and self-nominations for membership in the ACM REP '27 Artifact Evaluation Committee (AEC). We welcome researchers and practitioners interested in reproducibility who can help evaluate research artifacts and provide judgement. We expect nominees should be familiar with the research methods, software, or data relevant to the artifacts they would like to evaluate.

AEC should be available during the bidding, kick-the-tires, and main evaluation periods listed under <a class="js-scroll" href="#deadlines">important dates</a>, and for discussions leading to the final badge decisions. AEC are also expected to respond to authors and fellow reviewers in a timely manner, declare conflicts of interest, and maintain the confidentiality of submission materials.

The AEC will produce a report detailing the steps taken to reproduce artifacts and the results, which will be included in the conference proceedings. All AEC members who participate fully in the review process will be credited as co-authors.
