**Biography:**

 	
Miriam Leeser is a Professor in the [Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering](http://www.ece.neu.edu/) at [Northeastern University](http://www.neu.edu/). She received her BS degree in Electrical Engineering from Cornell University, and Diploma and Ph.D. Degrees in Computer Science from Cambridge University in England. After completion of her Ph.D., she joined the faculty of Cornell University, Department of Electrical Engineering. In January, 1996 she joined the faculty of Northeastern University, where she is Director of the Northeastern University Reconfigurable Computing Laboratory ([RCL](http://www.coe.neu.edu/Research/rcl/)) and a member of the Computer Engineering group. In 1992 she received an NSF Young Investigator Award. Throughout her career she has been funded by both government agencies and companies, and is currently funded by DARPA, the NSF, Google, and Mathworks. She has served on the Mathworks industrial advisory board for more than five years. She is associate editor of the ACM Transactions on Reconfigurable Systems, the EURASIP Journal on Embedded Systems, and the International Journal of Reconfigurable Computing. She is a senior member of ACM, a senior member of IEEE and a senior member of SWE. She was named a Fulbright Scholar in 2018: [Faster Connections on the Fly](https://news.northeastern.edu/2018/03/14/faster-connections-on-the-fly/).

Professor Leeser has worked on reproducibility and artifact evaluation for her research community for many years, and has recently published an [article detailing artifact evaluation in FPGA research](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3802561).
