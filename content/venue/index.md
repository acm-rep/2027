---
title: Venue
---

The conference will be held in the Price Engineering and Computing Building on the University of Utah campus.
This is a brand-new building opening in December 2026, located in the foothills
of the Wasatch Mountains about 10 miles from the Salt Lake City International
Airport and two miles from downtown Salt Lake City.

![Price Engineering and Computing Building](../media/Kohn-and-Marcia-Price-Computing-Engineering-Bldg-U-of-Utah-C01.jpg)


The building is fully ADA-compliant, and is equipped with mothers' rooms,
all-gender restrooms, and other modern accessibility features.
It is easily reachable via public transportation (5-minute walk from the University Medical [TRAX (light rail) stop](https://www.rideuta.com/)) and directly adjacent to a stop for the [free campus shuttle](https://uofubus.com/routes) (Warnock Engineering stop).

Additional details regarding venue logistics will be posted as the conference date nears.
